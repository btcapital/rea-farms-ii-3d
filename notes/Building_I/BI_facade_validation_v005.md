## 1. Exact cause of the facade corruption (from the v004 diagnosis, §1 of the control document)

The v002 overlay was generated by a 1-ft raster classification of the shaded elevation images. It produced 1,580 separate 0.10-ft boxes; 566 of them were smaller than 4 sq ft (cells around drawing tags, dimension text, leaders and hatch lines mis-classified as brick, panel or translucent wall), 195 boxes lay across window and door placeholders, the entrance received TWS1 and BRK1 boxes because the white PNL1 and white TWS1 textures are indistinguishable in the raster, 20 boxes floated on the vestibule plane 9 ft in front of the real wall (following the v001 CW1 placeholders there), boxes were created where no wall exists behind them (canopy area, parapet gaps), and every gap between boxes showed the neutral grey base wall. Overlay-on-overlay z-fighting was not a cause (0 overlapping coplanar pairs).
